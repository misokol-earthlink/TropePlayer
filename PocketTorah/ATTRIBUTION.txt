PocketTorah Audio and Timing Data
=================================

This directory contains selected data derived from the PocketTorah project.

PocketTorah was created by Russel Neiss and Rabbi Charlie Schwartz.

Original PocketTorah project:
https://github.com/rneiss/PocketTorah

The PocketTorah creators released the project's audio files and their
corresponding metadata under a Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike
(CC BY-SA) license.

This repository currently uses selected PocketTorah materials for Torah
audio playback, including:

    data/aliyah.json
    data/audio/
    data/torah/labels/

Only selected Torah readings are included. Haftarah audio and related
material are not included.

The Hebrew biblical text in data/torah/json/ is derived from the
Westminster Leningrad Codex (WLC), maintained by the J. Alan Groves
Center for Advanced Biblical Research.

The WLC text is public domain.

PocketTorah materials remain subject to their applicable original
license. No ownership of the PocketTorah recordings or associated
PocketTorah source material is claimed by this repository.
